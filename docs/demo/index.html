What it does

It hears the retakes and keeps the take you meant

The video from the screenshots, word for word. Struck-through words are what Voice Cut removed; nothing was added or regenerated.

  • The best take of every line

    It tells a corrected retake from a fluent but abandoned attempt, and keeps the one you meant.

  • Cuts only where it is silent

    Every cut lands between words, never inside one, and keeps each word's natural decay.

  • Natural pauses

    Each pause gets a length for its kind, and breaths and clicks become clean room tone.

  • Your real voice

    Nothing is synthesized. Every sound in the edit comes from your own recording.

  • Video too

    The picture follows the kept speech with direct cuts — no frozen frames, no fake pauses.

  • Runs on your computer

    Transcription, alignment and rendering are local. Only the planner request goes to the cloud.

1

Drop in a recording

WAV, MP3, M4A, FLAC, MOV, MP4 and other common formats. Each project is one .vc document you open from the Finder, your file manager or Explorer.

2

Press Voice Cut

Pick the planner, its model and the language, then press one button. Every stage shows its progress, and an interrupted run picks up where it stopped.

3

Fine-tune every cut

The edit opens as clips over the original recording, so nothing is ever lost.

  • Drag a clip edge to bring back removed words or trim them
  • Resize pauses, or press P to insert one
  • Split with S, delete, undo and redo
  • Listen at 0.5× to 2.5× while the caption follows every word

4

Export what you hear

MP4 or MOV video, or WAV, FLAC, AAC and MP3 audio. The preview is rendered by the same code as the export, so the file sounds exactly like the editor.

Planners

Choose the model that edits

The planner reads the transcript and chooses the takes. The list of models comes live from the provider, and you can type any other model name.

  • GeminiRecommended

    Hears the audio of every window as well as reading it, so it catches stumbles and mis-heard words.

  • OpenAISupported

    Reads the transcript of every window.

  • DeepSeekSupported

    Reads the transcript of every window.

  • Local (Ollama)Supported

    Open models on your Mac or PC, downloaded in the app. Nothing leaves the computer.

Your original file and any video never leave the computer. Keys live in the macOS Keychain, the Windows Credential Manager or your Linux keyring, and are sent only to their own provider.

How it works

Choose the takes, then cut only where it is silent

  1. 1

    Transcribe everything

    Whisper writes down every word, and speech it skipped is transcribed again on its own.

  2. 2

    Choose the takes

    The planner reads minutes at a time with look-ahead, and revokes a choice when a better retake comes.

  3. 3

    Find the real edges

    The Montreal Forced Aligner and the waveform decide where every kept word starts and stops.

  4. 4

    Fix the pauses

    Every gap gets a natural length; breaths and clicks become room tone from the same recording.

  5. 5

    Render once

    Kept speech is copied sample for sample and joined with short crossfades in silence.

Opening it the first time

  1. Allow the download

    The installers are not signed with paid certificates yet, so your system asks once. macOS: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway. Windows: More info › Run anyway. Linux: make the AppImage executable; if it does not start, install libfuse2.

  2. Install the engine

    One click sets up Python, PyTorch, Whisper, the aligner and FFmpeg: about 1.5 GB, once. Speech models, about 2 GB, follow with the first Voice Cut.

  3. Add a planner key

    Paste an API key in Settings — a Gemini key is all you need to start.

Questions

Is VoiceCut really free?

Yes. VoiceCut is open source under the MIT License, with no account and no subscription. A cloud planner uses your own API key, so its provider bills you for what Voice Cut sends it, under that provider's pricing.

macOS says VoiceCut can't be opened. What do I do?

The installers are not signed with a paid Apple certificate yet, so macOS stops the first launch of a downloaded copy. Drag VoiceCut to Applications first (a copy opened from the disk image cannot update itself), open it, and close the message. Then open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down to the note that VoiceCut was blocked, choose Open Anyway and confirm. You do this once. In Terminal, xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VoiceCut.app does the same.

And on Windows or Linux?

On Windows, SmartScreen says it protected your PC: choose More info › Run anyway. The installer needs no administrator. On Linux, make the AppImage executable (chmod +x) and run it; if it does not start on Ubuntu, install libfuse2 (libfuse2t64 on 24.04 and later). The .deb installs with sudo apt install ./VoiceCut-…-amd64.deb.

Does my recording leave my computer?

Transcription, alignment and rendering run locally. The planner receives the transcript of each planning window; Gemini also receives a compressed 16 kHz mono copy of that window's audio. Your original file and any video never leave the computer.

Which languages does it understand?

English and Russian, recorded by one main narrator. You choose the language in the toolbar before pressing Voice Cut.

Will it ever cut a word in half?

Cuts are placed where the waveform is silent, between words. Tightly connected speech, with no pause between a kept and a removed word, can occasionally sound abrupt, and the planner can occasionally choose the wrong take. Every cut can be adjusted on the timeline, so listen before you publish.

What does it need to run?

macOS 14 or later (Apple Silicon or Intel), Windows 10 or 11 on x64, or Linux on x86_64 with glibc 2.35+, and about 4 GB of disk for the engine. Whisper uses the GPU on Apple Silicon and on NVIDIA GPUs; elsewhere it runs on the CPU, about as fast as the recording plays.

How do updates work?

When a new version is out, a download button appears in the toolbar. By default the update downloads in the background and installs when you quit, and your projects open again after the restart. You can switch that off in Settings.

Is there a command line?

Yes: voicecut recording.wav runs the same pipeline from a source checkout. See the development guide.
